# Design

## Visual Direction

Blinkin should feel like a sovereign AI app platform for business teams: high-contrast, precise, confident, and product-led. The page alternates between dark Monitor-style bands and calm light workspace sections. Real platform screenshots carry the proof: Monitor feeds, article review, notebooks, signal evaluation, Knowledge Spaces, Studio, Table Review, Editor, Artifacts, companion apps, and runnable deployments.

## Color

Use a stark near-black and white system softened by pale yellow CTAs and quiet blue, lilac, and mint surface tints.

- Primary: near-black for the header, hero, Monitor-style bands, and strong CTA context.
- Secondary: near-black text on light sections.
- Tertiary: warm pale yellow for primary actions, dark-section emphasis, and selected state accents.
- Neutral: light product-section background.
- Surface: clean white cards, screenshots, forms, and product surfaces.
- Surface Blue, Surface Lilac, and Mint: quiet section transitions and operational states.
- Text Muted: secondary copy on light sections.
- Text Inverse Muted: secondary copy on dark sections.
- Accent Purple: sparse eyebrows and small brand moments only.

## Typography

Use Manrope as the intended single-family system, with the local sans stack as fallback. Headlines are clean, geometric, medium-weight, and direct. Use tight negative tracking only for large display copy. Body text should stay readable, usually 16-20px with medium weight. Button labels are uppercase, bold, and slightly tracked.

## Layout

The first screen should feel like a product command surface, not a marketing poster. Use an asymmetric hero: direct app-platform statement on the left, large real product screenshot on the right, and capability chips below. Major sections use a desktop working width around 1180px, with wider proof galleries and closed-loop diagrams when screenshots or workflow logic need room.

Use generous spacing between major sections. Product proof should be shown as large real interface imagery rather than abstract illustrations. Avoid generic icon grids. For module overviews, use structured lanes that map to actual platform areas: Knowledge, Builder, Review, Deployment, Governance, and Builder Core.

The closed-loop section should feel like the product thesis: trusted enterprise knowledge becomes workflow logic, workflow logic becomes a deployed app or surface, live signals become grounded output, expert review approves what ships, and only approved outcomes become durable memory.

## Components

Navigation is black with the current Blinkin wordmark, muted gray links, and a pale yellow CTA. Buttons use 8px radius. Product cards, workflow cards, trust-layer controls, and screenshot frames use 16-24px rounded corners. Tabs stay keyboard-accessible and scan-friendly. Screenshot captions should name the platform surface and the operational job it performs.

## Elevation

Prefer tonal contrast, screenshots, scale, soft section tints, and quiet borders over heavy shadows. Dark sections carry the emotional weight; light sections carry operational clarity.

## Motion

Use restrained reveal motion only where it helps hierarchy. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
